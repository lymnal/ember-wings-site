Getting started

  1. Install Ember Wings from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click the Ember Wings button in the toolbar. If you can't see it, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin it. The game opens in a tab.
  3. Choose Start the campaign. The tutorial teaches steering, scooping and dropping water.

Controls: steer with A/D or ←/→. Climb and descend with W/S or ↑/↓. Hold Space to drop water. Esc pauses, F toggles fullscreen, M mutes, and Enter skips radio chatter or the intro flyover. Touch screens show a joystick and a DROP button.

Troubleshooting

"Ember Wings needs 3D graphics" or a black screen. Open chrome://settings/system, turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, and restart Chrome. If it still fails, chrome://gpu shows whether WebGL is blocked on your device or by your organization.

The game is slow or choppy. Open Settings and choose Low quality. Plugging in a laptop and closing other heavy tabs also helps. With Lower quality automatically turned on, the game steps down by itself once per session if the frame rate stays low.

No sound. Click inside the game once (browsers block audio until you interact). Then check the volume sliders, the mute setting (M) and your system volume.

I can't scoop. Fly low over calm water: under 4.5 m on the altimeter. Some missions say the open sea is too rough; use the lakes or channels shown in light blue on the minimap.

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on this device and in this Chrome profile. It doesn't sync between computers, and uninstalling the extension deletes it. If saved data is ever unreadable, the game starts fresh and keeps a backup copy internally.

Premium

Buying. Choose Unlock the full campaign and then Buy Premium. Checkout opens on our payment provider's site (Polar). After paying, open Access purchase in your receipt email, or use Find my key (customer portal) in the game. Copy the licence key from the portal, paste it into the game's Premium screen, and press Activate.

Restoring on a new computer or after reinstalling. Paste the same licence key into Premium → Activate or restore a purchase. If you lost the key, use Find my key (customer portal) and sign in with the email you used at checkout.

"Could not reach the licence server." Check your connection and press Retry. Once a key is verified, Premium keeps working offline for up to 60 days between checks.

"This licence is no longer valid." This appears after a refund or a chargeback. If you think it's a mistake, contact us with your order email.

Refunds. Full refund on request within 30 days of purchase. Email ethanesame@gmail.com with your order email and invoice number to request one. After a refund, Premium locks again the next time the game checks your licence online.
