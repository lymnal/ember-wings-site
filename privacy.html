Ember Wings privacy policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026. Applies to the Ember Wings Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 and later, published by Ethan Sam ("we")._

Summary

Ember Wings is a single-player game that runs on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The game sends only one kind of request over the network: when you activate or re-check Premium, it sends your licence key to our payment provider to confirm the purchase.

Information stored on your device

The game stores the following in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Game progress and settings stay on your device. Your licence key is sent to Polar only for licence verification as described below:

  • game progress: missions completed, stars, best scores, daily-challenge and endless-mode records, story chapters seen;
  • settings: volumes, graphics quality, comfort and control options;
  • if you buy Premium: your licence key, whether it is active, and when it was last verified.

You can erase progress at any time in Settings → Reset local progress, and remove a licence in Premium → Remove licence from this device. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this data.

Licence verification

When you activate a licence key, and about once a day afterwards while you are online, the game sends the licence key and our store's public product identifiers to Polar (Polar Software Inc.), our payment provider and merchant of record. Polar replies whether the key is valid. We use this only to unlock Premium and to lock it again if a purchase is refunded. Polar receives your IP address as part of any internet request. Polar's privacy policy: Polar's privacy policy.

Purchases

Checkout happens on Polar's website, not inside the game. Polar collects the information needed to process your payment, such as your email address, payment details and billing country, under its own privacy policy and terms. We receive purchase records from Polar (for example, your email address and order details) so we can provide support, refunds and licence restoration. Order and licence records are kept in Polar; Polar controls their retention under its own privacy policy. We use our support email account to handle help requests. We delete routine support messages 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is needed for tax or other legal obligations, an unresolved dispute, or an ongoing support matter. We keep records required for those purposes only as long as necessary. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; some records may need to be retained for those obligations.

Product website

The product, support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to those pages. GitHub processes technical information needed to serve the website under its own privacy statement: GitHub privacy statement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, do not share it with data brokers, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. The use of information received from Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The game has no accounts and collects no personal information during play. Purchases are made on Polar's site, which applies its own age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the extension's store listing.
